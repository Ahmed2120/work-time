Privacy Policy

Ommali — The Workforce Management Companion for Business Owners

This Privacy Policy describes our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use the Ommali application, and tells you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

By using the application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Interpretation and Definitions

Definitions

  • Application: Refers to Ommali, the software program provided on Android devices.
  • Company: Refers to the developer of the Ommali application (referred to as "We", "Us", or "Our" in this agreement).
  • Device: Any device that can access the Service, such as a smartphone or tablet.
  • Personal Data: Any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.
  • Service: Refers to the Ommali application.
  • User: The individual or business owner who accesses or uses the application.
  • Country: Arab Republic of Egypt.

Data We Collect and How We Use It

1. User-Entered Data (Worker Data)

The application stores data that you personally enter to manage your workforce, including:

  • Worker names, job roles, and daily wage rates.
  • Daily attendance and absence records.
  • Advances and received payments.
  • Work locations and project sites.
  • Work-related notes.
✅ Important: All this data is stored locally on your device only in an encrypted database. We do not access or collect it on our servers at any time.

2. Google Account and Google Drive Backup

The application optionally allows users to link their Google account to create a backup of their data on Google Drive.

  • When this feature is enabled, the application requests access to an app-specific private folder only within Google Drive (drive.appdata scope), and cannot access any other files in your account.
  • The backup file is encrypted and uploaded to your own Drive storage.
  • We do not receive or store your Google Drive data on any external server.
  • You can revoke access permissions at any time from your Google account settings.

To learn how Google handles your data, please review: Google Privacy Policy.

3. License Activation Data (Firebase)

When you purchase or activate the application, the system verifies your email address via Google Firebase to validate your license. Your email address is used solely for the purpose of license verification.

  • License data (subscription and trial status) is stored encrypted on your device using Android KeyStore (Hardware-Backed Encryption), preventing any tampering.
  • We do not share your email address with any third party beyond the scope of license verification.

4. In-App Purchases, Free Trial, and Subscriptions (Google Play Billing)

All in-app subscription and purchase transactions are processed securely through the Google Play Billing system.

  • 14-Day Free Trial: New users receive a 14-day unrestricted free trial upon installation. No payment information is collected during the trial period. After 14 days, users can optionally subscribe to maintain full access.
  • Financial Information: Ommali does not collect, process, access, or store any credit card numbers, bank account details, or payment credentials. All payment processing is handled exclusively by Google Play.
  • Purchase Receipts: We only receive purchase confirmation tokens, product IDs, and transaction receipts from Google Play to verify your subscription validity and unlock premium features.

5. Usage Data

Ommali does not collect any analytics or automatic usage statistics. The application operates entirely in Offline-First mode and does not require a constant internet connection.

Daily Reminder Notifications

The application uses the local notification system on your device to send a daily reminder to log attendance. These notifications operate entirely locally without any external servers, and can be disabled at any time from your phone or app settings.

Sharing Data with Third Parties

We do not sell or share your data with any third parties for commercial or marketing purposes. The only data that leaves your device is:

  • The encrypted database file uploaded to your personal Google Drive folder when you request it.
  • Your email address used for license verification via Firebase.
  • Purchase receipt tokens communicated securely with Google Play to verify subscriptions.

Deleting Your Data

Since all your data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by:

  • Uninstalling the application from your device (deletes the local database).
  • Manually deleting the backup file from Google Drive if you created one.
  • Contacting us via the email address below to request deletion of any data associated with your account.

Security of Your Data

We take the security of your data seriously. We use the following measures to protect your information:

  • License data encryption using Android KeyStore with EncryptedSharedPreferences.
  • Backup data isolation in an app-specific folder on Google Drive inaccessible to any other application.
  • The application operates primarily without an internet connection, reducing the risk of unauthorized access.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we always strive to use the best available means to protect your data.

Children's Privacy

Ommali is designed exclusively for adults and business owners. The application does not target individuals under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you become aware that a child under 13 has used the application, please contact us immediately.

Links to Other Websites

The application may contain links to external websites (such as Google). We are not responsible for the privacy policies or content of those sites, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies.
